Cute illustrations that make you feel a little happier.

miponoe is a free illustration site featuring hand-drawn kawaii clipart of children, animals, seasonal events and more. All illustrations are drawn one by one with love, in soft and easy-to-match colors.

They are perfect for nursery and classroom wall decor, newsletters, social media, product labels and handmade crafts.
